Clip Desk Publisher

A small desktop tool that the operator of the GTA6 Crazy Clips pages runs on their own computer to edit short gaming clips and publish them to their own TikTok and Instagram accounts with the official APIs.

  • Turns longer gameplay videos into short vertical clips with captions and credit to the original creator.
  • Shows every clip on a review screen first: preview, title, privacy, comments/Duet/Stitch and commercial content disclosure.
  • Publishes only after the creator presses Publish.

Not offered to the public. Contact: message @gta6.crazy.clips on Instagram or TikTok.
